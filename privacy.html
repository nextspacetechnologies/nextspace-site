Privacy Policy

How NextSpace Technologies OPC collects, uses and protects personal information, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Who we are

NextSpace Technologies OPC (“NextSpace”, “we”, “us”) is based in Marikina City, Philippines. We operate this website and act as the personal information controller for the information described here.

What we collect

  • Details you submit through the inquiry form: full name, business email, contact number, company name, service required, estimated budget and project details.
  • Details you send us by email or phone.
  • Basic technical data, such as device type and pages visited, to keep the website working and improve it.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare quotations and proposals you request.
  • To keep our systems secure and prevent misuse.

Consent

We collect your information with your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box when you submit the inquiry form. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us operate, such as hosting, database and email providers. They may use it only to provide their service to us. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you, manage any resulting project, and meet legal and accounting requirements. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to be informed about how we use your data, access it, correct it, object to its processing, ask for it to be erased or blocked, receive a copy in a usable format, and claim damages for a violation. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Security

We use reasonable organizational, physical and technical measures to protect personal information. No system is perfectly secure. We will notify affected people and the authorities of a breach as the law requires.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email nextspacetechnologies.opc@gmail.com or write to NextSpace Technologies OPC, Marikina City, Philippines.
