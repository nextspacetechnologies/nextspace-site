FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does NextSpace Technologies do?
We build practical digital solutions that help businesses build, sell, operate and connect online: websites, sales funnels, software, NFC and QR products, business messaging, virtual tours, and creative production and printing through NextSpace Studio.
Who do you work with?
Any business, worldwide. We are based in Marikina City, Philippines.
What are NextSpace 360, NextSpace Go and NextSpace Studio?
They are the three brands NextSpace Technologies operates under. NextSpace 360 (virtual tours) is live. NextSpace Go (a 360° property marketplace) and NextSpace Studio (creative production and printing) are coming soon.
What is NextSpace Studio?
NextSpace Studio is our creative production arm: graphic design, photography and video, printing, apparel, corporate giveaways and event materials from one team.
Do you offer marketing services?
Yes. We help businesses get noticed, generate leads and win customers through social media, sales funnels, brochures, branded giveaways and automation.
How is pricing handled?
Every project and product is quoted to your requirements. Send us your goal and we will prepare a quotation, with VAT and optional add-ons confirmed in it.
How do we get started?
Send the inquiry form or email us your goal. If you are not sure where to start, tell us what you want to achieve and we will recommend the right mix and quote it.
